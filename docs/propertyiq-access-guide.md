# PropertyIQ Access Guide

Use this checklist after you purchase PropertyIQ.

1. Open the PropertyIQ access link.
2. Enter the email used at checkout.
3. Enter the unique licence key from your receipt or download page.
4. Activate access.
5. Bookmark PropertyIQ.

Licence keys are issued with Payhip and Gumroad purchases. Each licence is unique to your purchase. PropertyIQ does not use a shared bypass code. If you need to move to a new browser, sign out under Settings → PropertyIQ access, then activate again. Contact support if a legitimate device cannot activate.
